Four things a certificate will not tell you

The entity does not match

The company that signed your contract is not the named insured on the policy. Close name, different entity. On paper you are covered. In practice you are not.

The policy expired eight months in

A two-year project outlasts a twelve-month policy. If nobody chases the renewal, you are carrying the risk and you will not know until you need it.

The endorsement points at last year

Additional insured and waiver language that no longer attaches to anything current.

The financials were filed, not read

A subcontractor's numbers have been getting worse for three years. Somebody collected them every year. Nobody looked.

Questions about counterparty compliance

We already use COI tracking software. How is this different?

Tracking software confirms a certificate exists and the dates are current. That is useful and it is not the same as knowing you are covered. We read the policies behind the certificates and tell you where they do not match what your contracts require. Most clients keep their software. We are the judgment layer on top of it.

Do we have to move our insurance to you?

No. We handle counterparty risk management as its own engagement, whether or not we place your insurance. Plenty of clients start here and never move their program.

Who actually does the reviewing?

Licensed risk specialists. Documents get collected and organized systematically, which should be automatic, but every flag that reaches you has been checked by a person who can explain it.

We manage tenants, not subcontractors. Does this apply?

Yes, and the mechanics are close to identical. Lease requirements instead of subcontract requirements, certificates from tenants instead of subs. We also review the lease language itself, which is where we most often find exposure.

How long does onboarding take?

You can start with one project in a few days. A full program depends on how many counterparties you carry and what condition the documents are in.

What happens to the documents we send you?

They stay confidential and are used only for your review. We will sign your NDA if you have one.
