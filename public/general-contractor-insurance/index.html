Your broker should always understand the business behind the policy.

Insurance affects operations and finance, yet it is usually handled as a transaction. Your broker needs to understand how you operate, the market you work in, and the businesses you contract with. Without that, they cannot represent you properly to insurers, and work gets held up or lost.

Questions about working with InFrame

What does InFrame Risk do?

InFrame Risk is a construction and real estate insurance broker. We place and service insurance programs for specialty contractors, general contractors, and property owners, and we read the contracts, leases, and policies behind every certificate so you know what you’re covered for and who else you’re on the hook for.

What does a construction insurance broker do?

A construction insurance broker represents your business, not an insurance company. They assess your risk, take your program to multiple carriers, negotiate coverage and price, and service the account all year: certificates, endorsements, audits, and claims. A specialist broker also reviews the contracts that drive your insurance requirements.

How is InFrame different from a traditional insurance broker?

Most brokers focus on the renewal and treat certificates as paperwork. We start with how your business actually operates, including your contracts, subs, tenants, and entities, and build your program around that. Compliance tracking runs all year, and a licensed advisor checks the work.

Is InFrame an insurance broker or a software company?

A licensed insurance broker first. Our construction risk management platform is how we deliver the service: it collects and reviews contracts, certificates, and policies so problems surface before a claim does. You work with experienced construction insurance advisors, and the technology does the reading.

When is the right time to switch insurance brokers?

Four to six months before renewal is ideal, since complex programs take time to underwrite. If a claim, a compliance miss, or a premium spike has you questioning your current setup, you don’t have to wait: a broker of record letter can move servicing to InFrame mid-term without canceling your policies.
