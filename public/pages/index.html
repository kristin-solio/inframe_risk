The original black and white review document: all 26 modules, rationale notes, approval and verify flags, and the approval register. Untouched, so it still documents the retired gap report page and the single-CTA decision.

wireframe.html

The two Quality Score pages. The first two cards are the next version of the landing pages, built to fix the Below average “landing page experience” rating on the non-brand keywords. They reuse the same brand system, header, footer, legal fine print, Fillout embed, attribution script and Tag Manager container as the three pages below them. The band under the hero carries the offer in three numbered steps, over a licensing line drawn from the approved footer copy and public licence records. No client result appears on either page. Copy on both was confirmed on 12 Nov and the review tags have been removed.

How to review. These are the visitor-facing pages only. The internal rationale notes, approval and verify flags, module numbers and the approval register live in the wireframe document and are stripped from the pages by design.

Copy comes from three approved sources: the wireframe document, the homepage, and the FAQ sheet. The mid-market focus is stated in body copy only, not in any headline or hero lede, and each page answers it directly in an “Is InFrame a fit” FAQ, in words rather than revenue bands. Several claims are still unapproved, and every page is set to noindex until they clear.
